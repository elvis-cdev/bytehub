# ByteHub Kenya

## Mission

Help student developers prove their skills through real projects and connect them with companies looking for talent.

## Vision

Become Africa's leading platform for discovering and hiring emerging software developers.

## Primary Users

- University students
- Self-taught developers
- Startups
- SMEs
- Recruiters
- Software companies

## Core Value

Your work should speak louder than your résumé.

## MVP

- Authentication
- Developer profiles
- Project showcases
- Company accounts
- Hiring requests
- Search & filters
- ByteScore
